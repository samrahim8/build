FAQ

Questions we get

"My audience is professional. Won't games feel gimmicky?"

A retirement simulator doesn't feel like Candy Crush. We calibrate the tone to your market. The interactivity is the hook, not the gimmick.

A quiz is a form with personality. A Pop-Up is an interactive game designed to deliver a result worth sharing. People don't screenshot quiz results.

That's the point. Games get shared. One person plays, shares their result, brings three more. Your Pop-Up becomes a growth loop, not a dead end.

"Will it work with my email platform?"
