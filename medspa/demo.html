Both are neuromodulators that relax muscles to smooth wrinkles. Dysport spreads a bit more, making it great for larger areas like the forehead. Botox is more precise for smaller areas. Results are similar — it often comes down to personal preference.
At Lumière, Botox is $12–14 per unit. Most people need 20–30 units for the forehead and 10–15 units per eye area (crow's feet). A typical full treatment runs $250–450. We're transparent about pricing — no surprises.
