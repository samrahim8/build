What treatments can you include?

Where do the leads go?
Directly to your email (or your CRM). Each submission includes the visitor's name, contact info, and their complete treatment plan with estimated cost. High-intent leads, pre-qualified.
Can I update pricing later?
Yes. You get the source code. Any developer can update it — or you can, with basic HTML knowledge. We also offer a quarterly update package if you want us to handle it.
What about before/after photos?
You provide your own client photos (with consent). We design the gallery layout. Real results from your practice are the best social proof.
